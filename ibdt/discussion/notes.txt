IB Design Tech v2 — Learning Flow
==================================

Inspired by 6 Hats Discussion (learn/6hats/6hatsdiscussion). Standalone IB DT
curriculum app — no links to external legacy folders.

Recommended order for each topic:

  1. Read the passage (articleText)
  2. Do the fill-in-the-blank exercise (vocabex)
  3. Answer the 6 thinking-lens questions

Why this works
--------------

Reading first gives learners context — facts, design ideas, and vocabulary in
full sentences. Fill-in-the-blank checks recall of key DT terms. The 6
questions push deeper thinking from different angles:

  - White  — facts & definitions (syllabus recall)
  - Red    — user feelings & experience
  - Yellow — benefits & advantages
  - Black  — problems, risks & limitations
  - Green  — design ideas & innovation
  - Blue   — process, planning & next steps

Flow: input → recall → application & discussion.

What each topic contains
------------------------

  - articleText   — reading passage (handcrafted or generated)
  - vocablist     — key terms with short Chinese hints
  - vocabex       — fill-in-the-blank sentences
  - questions     — 6 lens-tagged discussion prompts
  - Wikipedia     — supplementary reading link per topic

Topic tiers
-----------

  - Full topics (topics/*.js) — hand-crafted reading, blanks, and questions
  - Vocab topics (auto-built) — generated from keyvocab-data.js
  - Overview topics (topic-readings.js) — generated reading + Wikipedia link

Navigation
----------

  - index.html — search bar only; loads Topic 1 (Human Factors) by default
  - subjectbrief.html, dtsl.html, dthl.html — clean syllabus guides in ibv2/
  - Guide pages link to index.html?topic=slug

Vocab display
-------------

  - Click a key term to reveal Chinese (HK) translation
  - English definitions stay in the reading passage only

Suggested use in class
----------------------

  - Assign the reading before class or at the start of the lesson.
  - Students complete blanks individually or in pairs.
  - Use the 6 questions for pair work, oral practice, or design journal prompts.
  - For HL topics, use the Wikipedia link and six questions for deeper discussion.
