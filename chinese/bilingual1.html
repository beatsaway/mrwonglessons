The Butcher and the Ox (庖丁解牛)

Part 1 - The Amazing Sight ｜ 第一部分 - 驚人的場面

One day, Lord Wenhui watched a butcher carving up an ox. 有一天，文惠君看見一位庖丁（廚師）在宰牛。
The butcher touched the ox with his hand, braced it with his shoulder, stepped with his foot, and pressed with his knee. 他用手觸摸牛身，用肩膀倚靠，用腳踩踏，用膝蓋頂推。
Every movement made a sound, and every sound was perfectly in rhythm. 每一個動作都會發出聲響。每一個聲響都完全合拍。
It sounded like a graceful piece of music. 聽起來就像一首優美的樂曲。
Lord Wenhui exclaimed, "Remarkable! How did you become so skilful?" 文惠君驚訝地說：「哇！你的技術怎麼能達到這樣的地步？」

Part 2 - The Butcher's Secret ｜ 第二部分 - 庖丁的秘密

The butcher set down his knife and said: 庖丁放下他的刀，說道：
"What I pursue is the Way (Dao), which goes beyond technique." 「我所追求的是『道』，這已經超越了技術。」
"When I first began butchering oxen, I could only see the whole animal." 「我剛開始宰牛的時候，眼裡看見的是一整頭牛。」
"After three years, I no longer saw it as a whole ox." 「三年之後，我再看見的不是一整頭牛了。」
"Now, I engage with the ox through my spirit, not through my eyes." 「現在，我是用心神去接觸牛，而不是用眼睛去看。」

Part 3 - Why His Knife Never Gets Dull ｜ 第三部分 - 為何他的刀永不變鈍

"A good butcher changes his knife every year because he chops." 「好的廚師每年換一把刀，因為他們用砍的。」
"An average butcher changes his knife every month because he hacks." 「普通的廚師每月換一把刀，因為他們用剁的。」
"But I have used this knife for nineteen years, and it has cut thousands of oxen." 「而我的刀已經用了十九年，宰過數千頭牛。」
"Yet the blade is still as sharp as if it had just been ground." 「但刀鋒仍然像剛磨好的一樣鋒利。」
"Why? Because I cut through the gaps between the bones." 「為什麼呢？因為我是順著骨頭之間的空隙去切。」
"The blade is thin, and the gaps are spacious." 「刀鋒沒有厚度，而空隙有足夠的空間。」
"So the edge never strikes bone." 「所以刀鋒從來不會碰到骨頭。」
"That is why, after nineteen years, my knife is still like new." 「這就是為什麼我的刀用了十九年仍然像新的一樣。」

Part 4 - The Lesson ｜ 第四部分 - 當中的道理

Lord Wenhui said: 文惠君說：
"Excellent! By listening to this butcher, I have learned how to live well." 「妙極了！我聽了一位廚師說話，卻學會了如何養生（處世）。」

Discussion Questions 討論問題

  1. What does the butcher mean by saying he follows the "Way (Dao)" and not only skill?
    庖丁說他追求的是「道」而不只是技術，這句話是什麼意思？
  2. How can "cutting through gaps" be used as a metaphor for solving problems in life?
    「順著空隙去切」如何比喻我們在生活中處理問題的方法？
  3. Why do you think Lord Wenhui says he learned how to live from a butcher?
    你認為文惠君為何會說自己從廚師身上學到「養生（處世）」？
  4. Can you think of a real-life situation where doing something gently and precisely works better than forcing it?
    你能想到生活中哪種情況是「順勢而為」比「硬碰硬」更有效嗎？
  5. Which line in this story do you like most, and why?
    這篇故事中你最喜歡哪一句？為什麼？
